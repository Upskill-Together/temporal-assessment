Why you can trust it

Built so it can't double-book or get stuck

Only one “yes” can win

Only the client currently holding the offer, within their time, can accept. A second or late “yes” is turned away automatically, so last month's Saturday mix-up can't happen.

It keeps moving on its own

The 15-minute countdown runs behind the scenes, not on anyone's screen. It moves to the next client even if no one is watching, and picks up where it left off if a computer restarts.

Everyone hears back

The booked client and front desk get a confirmation. Anyone who timed out or was skipped is told, and stays on the waitlist.

You stay in control

Skip, book by hand or cancel at any point. There's always a clear record of who was contacted and what happened.

Powered by Temporal. Each opening runs as its own process that remembers exactly where it is: who has the offer, when their time runs out, and who's next. It recovers from failures automatically instead of relying on someone to remember.

Where the prototype stands

What's working, what's simulated, and a practical next step

Working now

  • Matching and ordering from the waitlist
  • One offer at a time, 15-minute reply window
  • Stops 1–2 hours before the appointment
  • Accept or decline from a phone, no account
  • Staff view with skip, book by hand, cancel
  • No double-booking

Simulated

  • Text messages and front-desk alerts are shown on screen, not actually sent
  • The waitlist is sample data, not your Google Sheet
  • Bookings are still added to Square by hand

Not included yet

  • Adding clients to the waitlist from the website
  • Staff sign-in
  • Automatic Square booking

Next step: a two-week pilot for same-day openings

  • Connect your real Google Sheet and send real text messages.
  • Carla runs every same-day cancellation through it.
  • Review together: how many openings were refilled (goal: half or more) and how much chasing it saved.
  • Decide how long clients should get for openings a few days out. You weren't sure yet.
